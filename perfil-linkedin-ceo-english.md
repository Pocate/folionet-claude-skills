## ABOUT

I've spent over 20 years in banking, finance, and investment management — at institutions including VectorGlobal WMG, Standard Chartered Bank, and Amerant Bank, before founding Folionet.

I founded Folionet because I saw a real gap: millions of Latin Americans without clear, regulated access to the world's largest investment market. Today we operate as a fully digital financial institution, regulated by the SEC and FINRA, with SIPC protection of up to $500,000 per client.

We serve clients in 65 countries. We've been operating for over 11 years. More than $7 billion in assets traded. Our clients range from first-time investors to Fortune 500 executives across Latin America — all investing in U.S. stocks, ETFs, and Treasury bonds, whether self-directed or supported by our advisory team.

I hold a Law degree, an MBA in Finance from Florida International University, and a Master of Laws from George Washington University — but what's shaped me most are nearly two decades of real conversations with Latin American investors.

My goal is simple: to make Folionet the most trusted and accessible gateway to the U.S. market for the Spanish-speaking investor.

If you have capital that isn't working as hard as it should be, let's talk.
