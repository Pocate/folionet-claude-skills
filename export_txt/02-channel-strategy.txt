## THE HONEST REALITY FIRST

Seven channels maintained by one non-specialist is not a strategy. It is maintenance. Maintenance produces noise, not growth.

The recommendation: run two channels properly, automate two, pause three. Add WhatsApp — but only with the right infrastructure.

---

## CHANNEL DECISIONS

### ✅ RUN PROPERLY — Full attention, original content

**LinkedIn (CEO personal brand)**
Why: This is where the ICP is. Directors, VPs, Founders, entrepreneurs. LinkedIn organic reach for personal accounts is significantly higher than company pages. The CEO speaking directly to the ICP builds trust faster than any brand campaign.
What to publish: 2x/week. Educational posts, data breakdowns, CEO perspective, category definition for Cuentas Asesoradas.
Who runs it: CEO writes or approves. AI (Claude + folionet-content skill) generates drafts. One review step. Publish.
Output expected: 10-15 new qualified followers/week → newsletter subscribers → discovery calls.

**Email (Mailchimp — existing 63K list)**
Why: 63,000 owned contacts is the most valuable asset Folionet has. No algorithm. No platform risk. Direct line to people who already raised their hand.
What to send: 1x/week. Educational newsletter. One topic, one CTA, one link. Never more than 300 words.
Who runs it: AI drafts from weekly trend research. Designer formats. One approval. Send.
Output expected: 2-3% weekly click rate → consistent discovery call traffic.

---

### ⚙️ AUTOMATE — Template and repurpose, minimal new effort

**Instagram**
Why keep it: Latin American audience is on Instagram. But not for financial research — for identity and aspiration. Use it to reinforce brand aesthetics, not to educate.
What to publish: Repurpose LinkedIn posts as carousels (1x/week) + market data visuals (1x/week). Designer already on team — this is their primary deliverable.
Who runs it: Designer repurposes LinkedIn content. No original strategy needed.
What not to do: Don't try to drive discovery calls from Instagram. Drive newsletter signups.

**WhatsApp (add — with conditions)**
Why add it: WhatsApp is the dominant communication channel across Venezuela, Colombia, Argentina, Mexico. It is where the ICP actually reads messages. Ignoring it is a structural gap.
The condition: Folionet must be FINRA-compliant on WhatsApp. This means:
- Opt-in only broadcast lists (never cold outreach)
- All messages reviewed before sending (or templated and pre-approved)
- No investment recommendations in group or broadcast format
- Record-keeping of all business communications (FINRA requirement)
How to use it compliantly:
- Post-discovery call follow-up (templated, pre-approved)
- Market event alerts for existing clients (educational, not advisory)
- Reactivation of warm leads who opted in
Who runs it: CEO or one designated team member. Not automated — personal.
Start date: Month 2, after compliance review of templates.

---

### ⏸️ PAUSE — Not wrong, just not now

**X (Twitter)**
Why pause: X's algorithm now heavily favors paid promotion. Organic financial content in Spanish reaches a fraction of the audience LinkedIn does. One-person team cannot maintain quality across both.
When to revisit: If a market crisis creates real-time commentary opportunity — respond then, not as a maintained channel.

**Facebook**
Why pause: Folionet's ICP (35-54 professional LATAM) uses Facebook personally, not professionally. Financial content does not perform organically on Facebook without paid amplification.
When to revisit: If budget exists for paid Facebook/Meta ads targeting by country + income level — that is a different conversation (paid media strategy, Month 3+).

**YouTube**
Why pause now: Long-form video requires scripting, recording, editing — skills and time the current team does not have.
When to invest: Month 3-4, once the CEO has a podcast format (already planned) and recorded episodes can be repurposed as YouTube content.
The bridge: LinkedIn video (short, 60-90 seconds, no editing) can test video resonance before committing to YouTube production.

## THE WHATSAPP RECOMMENDATION IN FULL

The document correctly identifies WhatsApp as a notable gap. Here is the full point of view:

**Yes — add WhatsApp. But not yet and not as a mass channel.**

WhatsApp belongs in the Folionet stack for three specific use cases:
1. Post-discovery call: send the CA one-pager automatically after every CEO call
2. Market events: alert existing clients to relevant market news (educational)
3. Warm lead reactivation: reach out personally to leads who went cold

What WhatsApp is NOT for: prospecting, cold outreach, or broadcasting to unqualified lists. In the current regulatory environment, unsolicited financial communication via WhatsApp creates compliance exposure.

**The setup required before activating:**
- WhatsApp Business account
- FINRA-compliant message templates reviewed by CCO
- Opt-in mechanism (Typeform pre-call qualifier works)
- Communication archiving tool (required by FINRA for all business communications)

**Recommended tool:** WhatsApp Business API via Twilio or similar — provides the archiving FINRA requires. Not the free WhatsApp Business app.

**Timeline:** Propose templates to CCO in Week 3. Activate in Month 2.
