## EXERCISE 2 — ICP Prioritization

```
Reference /marketing-strategy for company context.

Help me prioritize Folionet's ICP segments. Here are the 3 
profiles we've identified:

1. El Empresario LATAM — 40-54, owns a business in Venezuela/
   Colombia/Argentina, $50K–$300K in capital, wants to diversify 
   into US assets, motivated by currency risk and inflation

2. El Ejecutivo Corporativo — 35-50, Director/VP at multinational 
   or large LATAM company, $20K–$100K accumulated, no time to 
   manage investments himself, values sophistication and privacy

3. El Profesional LATAM en el Exterior — 35-50, Venezuelan or 
   Colombian living in Spain/Panama/Miami, stable income in euros 
   or dollars, wants US market exposure without active management

Platform data: 69.7% male, 30.3% female. Age breakdown: 
35-44 (38.1%), 45-54 (26%), 25-34 (22.9%). Top countries: 
Venezuela 34.1%, Argentina 12.8%, USA 12.4%, Colombia 10.7%.

For each segment give me:
- Maturity level: proven/core, scaling, testing, or not a priority
- Recommended time allocation this quarter (% of effort)
- Which product maps to them: self-directed or Cuenta Asesorada

Output a prioritized table and save it to /marketing-strategy.
```

## EXERCISE 3 — Marketing Advantages

```
Reference /marketing-strategy for company context.

Help me audit Folionet's marketing advantages — the unique 
catalysts or strengths that could accelerate growth when we 
lean into them.

Here are the advantages I think we have:

PRODUCT: Dual-regulated structure (FINRA broker-dealer + SEC RIA) 
— no competitor in LATAM advisory space has this. Cuenta 
Asesorada as a product category that doesn't exist elsewhere 
in Spanish-language content.

ECOSYSTEM: 63K owned email list — no algorithm dependency. 
Operating since 2014 with established trust in LATAM community.

CONTENT GAP: Nobody owns "Cuentas Asesoradas" as a content 
category in Spanish. AEO/GEO opportunity — AI currently cites 
nobody for "cómo invertir en bolsa americana desde Venezuela."

COMPETITOR WEAKNESS: Hapi.trade (most direct competitor) is 
mobile-first, Instagram-dependent, no advisory product, no 
newsletter infrastructure.

For each advantage:
- Strength today (1-10)  
- Potential when fully activated (1-10)
- Does Hapi.trade or any direct competitor have the same? Y/N
- Combination potential with other advantages?

Output a table and save it to /marketing-strategy.
```

## EXERCISE 4 — Perceptions

```
Reference /marketing-strategy for company context including 
ICP and advantages.

Help me define 3-5 perceptions for Folionet — the key narratives 
we want the ICP to hold. Written from the audience's POV, as 
beliefs they'd repeat back to us.

Context:
- The ICP currently perceives US investing as "for Americans" 
  or "too complicated for me"
- They don't know what a Cuenta Asesorada is — nobody has 
  explained it to them in Spanish
- They trust FINRA/SEC regulation but don't know Folionet has it
- Main competitor (Hapi) positions as a DIY trading app — 
  Folionet's opportunity is the advisory/expert-guided lane
- Compliance constraint: no specific return promises, no 
  client testimonials without approval

For each perception give me:
- The belief statement (written as the ICP would say it)
- Category: company insight, market insight, or product insight
- Status: brand new, emerging, or already established
- Which content pillar or campaign reinforces it

Output a table of 3-5 perceptions and save to /marketing-strategy.
```

## EXERCISE 5 — Positioning

```
Reference /marketing-strategy for company context.

Help me define Folionet's positioning. Answer these 4 questions:

1. Who is it for? 
2. What is it?
3. What are we comparing it to? (the alternative the ICP 
   currently uses)
4. Why is it better?

Important constraints:
- Primary focus is Cuenta Asesorada, not self-directed brokerage
- The ICP doesn't compare us to other brokers — they compare 
  us to "doing nothing" or "leaving money in a local bank"
- Our differentiator is expert management + US regulation, 
  not price or features
- Avoid generic fintech copy: 
  "transform your financial future," "start your journey"

Give me two versions:
- Positioning today (what we can credibly say now)
- Positioning in 12 months (where we're building toward)

Output a positioning table and save to /marketing-strategy.
```

## EXERCISE 6 — Revenue Levers

```
Reference /marketing-strategy for company context.

Help me stack-rank Folionet's 4 revenue levers for the next 
90 days:

1. New logos — core audience (LATAM professionals, 
   Cuenta Asesorada)
2. New logos — new audience (expanding to new segments)
3. Increase customer value (upsell self-directed users 
   to Cuenta Asesorada, increase AUM per client)
4. Increase efficiency (retention, reduce churn, lower CAC)

Context:
- LinkedIn + email strategy is the channel focus for Month 1
- 63K email subscribers already exist — activation and 
  upsell potential is high
- Discovery call is the primary conversion event
- App download is the secondary conversion event

For each lever:
- Priority rank 1-4
- Current investment level: active, just starting, or none
- How marketing specifically moves this lever in Month 1

Output a ranked table and save to /marketing-strategy.
```
