# folionet-webinar
**Version:** v0 — built from folionet-icp, folionet-voice, and the CA one-pager. Topic bank is drawn from ICP pain points and fears; update once real ticket-theme data and audience Q&A exist to sharpen it.

## What this skill does
Load this when planning, briefing, or promoting a monthly webinar. It defines the recurring format, the topic rotation, and the follow-up sequence — so each month's session doesn't have to be reinvented.

---

## THE FORMAT: "MERCADO ESTE MES"
**Length:** 30 minutes. 20 min content, 10 min Q&A.
**Cadence:** Monthly.
**Host:** CEO (matches the founder-authority pillar in the LinkedIn strategy — this is not a generic company webinar).
**Registration gate:** Substack (or the active newsletter platform, pending the CEO's channel decision) — this doubles as newsletter growth, not just event registration.
**Tone:** Same as `folionet-voice` — sophisticated, direct, never salesy. The webinar teaches; it doesn't pitch until the very end.

**Structure every session:**
1. Open with one current, real market data point relevant to LATAM investors (Data Breakdown pillar, live version)
2. What it means for someone sitting on idle capital in [country/region focus of the month]
3. One myth or misconception addressed directly
4. Q&A (10 min)
5. Soft close: one sentence inviting a discovery call — never a hard pitch mid-webinar

## PRE-EVENT PROMOTION SEQUENCE
- **3 weeks out:** Announce topic + date on LinkedIn (CEO post) and in the newsletter. Registration link goes live.
- **1 week out:** Reminder post + email. Include one question from the topic as a hook ("¿Sabes qué documento realmente necesitas para abrir una cuenta en EE.UU.?").
- **1 day out:** Final reminder — email + LinkedIn story/post if the format supports it. Direct, short: date, time, one-line value.

---

## POST-WEBINAR FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE
1. **Same day:** Thank-you email to attendees with the recording link + the CA one-pager attached.
2. **Same day, no-shows:** "Sorry we missed you" email with the recording link — no guilt framing, just the value.
3. **Day 3:** Soft discovery-call CTA email, referencing the specific topic covered ("Si la sesión sobre [tema] te dejó pensando en tu propia situación, conversemos 20 minutos.").
4. **Day 7:** If no call booked, one final low-pressure touch — a related LinkedIn post or newsletter mention, not a direct ask.

## COMPLIANCE NOTE
Live Q&A carries more compliance risk than scripted content — the CEO may get asked about specific returns or be tempted to reassure with a number. Before each session, remind the host: no specific performance figures, no forward-looking promises, hypothetical scenarios only if needed. Route anything uncertain to "let's cover that on your discovery call" rather than answering live.
