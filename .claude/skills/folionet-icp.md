# folionet-icp
**Version:** v1 — updated with real documents: onboarding guide, brand presentations, Metricool data, captions.

## What this skill does
Load this before writing any LinkedIn post, email, CTA, or carousel brief for Folionet. It defines exactly who you're talking to so every piece of content speaks to the right person and no one else.

---

## THE ONE-LINE ICP
The Latin American professional or entrepreneur who saves but doesn't invest — and now wants their capital working in the U.S. market with expert guidance and real regulatory protection.

---

## THE CORE NARRATIVE (from Folionet's own presentations)

**"El gran error latino"** — this is Folionet's framing of the problem their ICP has:
- Keeps all savings in bank accounts (loses to inflation)
- Long-term invests only in real estate (local or U.S.)
- Speculates: crypto, small businesses, forex
- Informal investments: loans without formal agreements
- LATAM currencies have suffered major depreciations
- Doesn't diversify — lives day to day

**"La envidia de los latinos: el sueño americano"** — what the ICP aspires to:
- Americans retire at 65-70, fully stop working
- They live off investment account income
- They're financially independent, maintain their lifestyle
- The reason: the U.S. is the richest society because it has the most people with investment accounts

**The insight Folionet uses:** "¿Por qué los latinos no invierten en cuentas de inversión? Cultura. Ignorancia."

This framing is powerful and compliance-safe. Use it.

## THE 3 PROFILES

### Profile 1 — El Empresario LATAM (Primary CA target)
- **Age:** 40-54
- **Location:** Venezuela, Colombia, Argentina, Spain, Miami
- **Situation:** Owns a business. Has $50K–$300K in cash or local assets accumulated over years.
- **Pain:** Currency risk, inflation, local political instability. Money sitting idle or losing value.
- **"El gran error" they make:** All savings in bank accounts + real estate only
- **Want:** Diversify into U.S. dollars and American assets. Someone trustworthy to manage it.
- **Entry point:** Referral from a colleague, CEO LinkedIn post, Instagram content
- **Decision trigger:** Trust. Regulatory backing. A real conversation with a human.

### Profile 2 — El Ejecutivo Corporativo
- **Age:** 35-50
- **Location:** Colombia, Mexico, Chile, Argentina
- **Situation:** Director/VP at a multinational or large LATAM company. High salary, annual bonuses. $20K–$100K accumulated without a clear investment strategy.
- **Pain:** Knows the U.S. market exists. Doesn't know how to access it as a foreigner. No time to research.
- **"El gran error" they make:** Money in bank account + occasional crypto speculation
- **Want:** Sophistication, privacy, and someone who handles it. Not a trading app — an advisor.
- **Entry point:** LinkedIn, colleague referral, Instagram educational content
- **Decision trigger:** Professional credibility of the platform. FINRA/SEC regulation.

### Profile 3 — El Profesional LATAM en el Exterior
- **Age:** 35-50
- **Location:** Venezuelan or Colombian living in Spain, Panama, or Miami
- **Situation:** Stable income in euros or dollars. Wants U.S. market exposure.
- **Pain:** Knows S&P 500 exists. Doesn't want to actively manage a portfolio.
- **"El gran error" they make:** Keeps savings in bank accounts despite earning in stronger currency
- **Want:** Someone to do it for them with real regulatory backing.
- **Entry point:** Google search in Spanish, Instagram, YouTube, referral.
- **Decision trigger:** Understanding the process clearly + regulatory trust.

## WHAT ALL 3 HAVE IN COMMON
- Capital between $20K–$500K sitting idle in bank accounts or local assets
- Spanish as primary language
- Distrust of local financial institutions
- **They save but don't invest** — this is the core insight from Folionet's own positioning
- Want U.S. dollar and U.S. market exposure
- **Do NOT want to become traders.** Want a trusted expert to manage it.
- Make decisions based on trust and referrals — not ads
- Value security, expertise, and regulatory protection above all

## WHAT THEY FEAR
- Sending money to a platform they don't understand
- Regulatory issues blocking their account
- Losing money in a market they can't monitor
- Their accountant or family thinking they're being irresponsible
- Crypto-level volatility (they've seen it, they're scared of it)

## WHAT TRIGGERS REGISTRATION
- A colleague who already uses Folionet and recommends it
- Instagram/LinkedIn content that makes them feel "esto fue hecho para mí"
- A local economic crisis (devaluation, inflation spike) that pushes them to diversify
- A post that explains "el gran error latino" and they recognize themselves in it

## WHAT TRIGGERS FIRST OPERATION
- A personal conversation where someone explains the process step by step
- Understanding exactly what happens to their money
- Knowing their account is protected by SIPC for up to $500,000
- Feeling the regulatory backing (FINRA/SEC) is real and verifiable

## HOW TO OPEN AN ACCOUNT (from real onboarding guide)
This is what the ICP goes through — content should reference this process naturally:
1. Email + password
2. Email verification code
3. Choose account type: Personal (self-directed) or Asesorada
4. Personal data + ID document
5. Country of residence (50+ countries supported — SSN/ITIN only if U.S. resident)
6. Employment status
7. Income + investor profile
8. Deposit method: U.S. bank, international wire transfer, debit card, or transfer from another broker

**Key insight for content:** The process is fully remote. No U.S. residency required. No SSN required for non-U.S. residents. Debit card and international wire both work. This removes the main objections.

## WHO IS NOT THE CLIENT
- Low-capital users without an investor profile
- The mass YouTube audience that wants to start with $100
- Crypto-first audience
- Users who generate high support volume without proportional revenue
- People who want to day trade or speculate

---

## CONTENT RULES WHEN USING THIS SKILL
- Frame the problem as "el gran error latino" — saves but doesn't invest
- Connect the solution to "the American dream" / how Americans build wealth
- Never write for "everyone" — write for Profile 1, 2, or 3 specifically
- Never use trading language (charts, technical analysis, buy/sell signals)
- Always speak to the desire for expert management, not self-empowerment
- Always tie back to security, trust, and regulatory protection (SIPC $500K is a real trust signal)
- Tone: sophisticated but warm. Like a trusted advisor, not a salesperson.
