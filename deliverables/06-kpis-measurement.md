# Folionet — Measurement Framework & KPIs
**Prepared by:** Isabella Pocaterra, creator:you  
**Date:** June 2026  
**North star metric:** First operations per month (activated clients who complete their first investment)

---

## THE FUNNEL — 6 STAGES WITH METRICS

### Stage 1 — AWARENESS
*Someone discovers Folionet exists*

| Metric | What it measures | Realistic benchmark (Month 1-3) |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------------------|
| LinkedIn CEO followers growth | Brand reach expansion | +50-100/week |
| LinkedIn post impressions | Content reach | 500-2,000/post |
| LinkedIn post engagement rate | Content resonance | 3-5% (above 2% = good for LinkedIn) |
| Organic search traffic (folionet.com) | SEO / AEO traction | Baseline Month 1, +20% Month 3 |

**Leading indicator:** Are new people finding the CEO's LinkedIn? If yes, the top of the funnel is working.

---

### Stage 2 — CAPTURE
*Someone gives Folionet their email*

| Metric | What it measures | Realistic benchmark |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Newsletter signups/week | LinkedIn → email conversion | 20-50/week (from LinkedIn) |
| Signup source breakdown | Which channel converts best | Track UTM by channel |
| Typeform pre-call completions | Intent signal | 10-20% of email subscribers |

**Leading indicator:** Are LinkedIn followers becoming email subscribers? If not, the bio link CTA needs work.

---

### Stage 3 — CONSIDERATION
*Someone reads the emails and thinks "maybe this is for me"*

| Metric | What it measures | Realistic benchmark |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Email open rate | Subject line + sender trust | 35-45% (LATAM finance average: 28%) |
| Email click rate | CTA effectiveness | 3-5% |
| Email 3 (bridge) click rate | Discovery call intent | 5-8% |
| Unsubscribe rate | Content relevance | <1% per email |

**Leading indicator:** Is Email 3 click rate above 3%? If yes, the sequence is converting. If no, the bridge message needs work.

---

### Stage 4 — INTENT
*Someone books a discovery call*

| Metric | What it measures | Realistic benchmark |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Discovery calls booked/week | Bottom-funnel conversion | 5-15/week (Month 1-3) |
| Call show rate | Lead quality | 70-80% |
| Calls → account opened | Sales conversion | 30-50% |
| Time from signup to call | Funnel velocity | Target: under 14 days |

**Leading indicator:** Discovery calls booked per week. This is the most important number after the north star.

---

### Stage 5 — ACTIVATION
*Someone opens an account and makes their first investment*

| Metric | What it measures | Realistic benchmark |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Accounts opened/month | Volume conversion | Track vs. baseline |
| Accounts opened → first operation | Activation rate | Target: 60%+ within 30 days |
| **First operations/month** | **NORTH STAR** | Set baseline Month 1 |
| AUM per new client | Client quality | Track vs. target profile |
| CA accounts vs. self-directed | Premium conversion | Target: 30%+ CA |

**Leading indicator:** First operations per month. If someone opens an account and never invests, the onboarding experience has a problem — not the marketing.

---

### Stage 6 — RETENTION & REFERRAL
*Someone stays and brings others*

| Metric | What it measures | Realistic benchmark |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Monthly active users (MAU) | Engagement | Track vs. baseline |
| Average AUM per client (growth) | Deepening relationship | +10-20%/quarter for CA |
| Referral rate | Client satisfaction | Target: 15-20% of new clients referred |
| NPS or CSAT | Client experience | Run quarterly — target 50+ NPS |
| Self-directed → CA upgrade rate | Upsell effectiveness | Track after Month 3 |

**Leading indicator:** Referral rate. The clients who bring other clients are Folionet's most powerful marketing channel — and currently the most underused.

## THE WEEKLY DASHBOARD — MINIMUM VIABLE TRACKING

Every Monday, before running the content research, check these 5 numbers:

1. **New LinkedIn followers** (this week vs. last week)
2. **New email subscribers** (this week vs. last week)
3. **Email open rate** (last email sent)
4. **Discovery calls booked** (this week)
5. **First operations** (this month to date)

That's it. Five numbers. If they're trending up: continue. If one drops: investigate before changing the content.

---

## THE MONTHLY REVIEW — WHAT TO ASK

**End of Month 1:**
- Which LinkedIn posts got the most engagement? What do they have in common?
- Which email subject lines had the highest open rates?
- How many discovery calls came from LinkedIn vs. email vs. referral?
- What % of new accounts are CA vs. self-directed?
- What are the top 3 questions people ask on the discovery call?

**End of Month 3:**
- Is the ICP hypothesis (3 profiles) validated by who's actually opening accounts?
- Is the CA positioning resonating? (measure: % of calls that mention "Cuenta Asesorada" specifically)
- Which channel has the best cost-per-acquisition? (even if all are organic today)
- Has the referral rate moved? What's driving it?

---

## WHAT NOT TO TRACK (yet)

- Vanity metrics: total Instagram followers, total Facebook likes
- Metrics that require paid media to matter: CPM, ROAS (not relevant until Month 3+)
- YouTube metrics (channel is paused)
- X/Twitter metrics (channel is paused)
