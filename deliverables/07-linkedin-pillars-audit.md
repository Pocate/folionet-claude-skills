## PART 2 — THE 4 CONTENT PILLARS

Derived directly from `folionet-icp` (what the 3 profiles fear, want, and need to hear) and `folionet-voice` (sophisticated-advisor tone, never salesy). Every post should map to exactly one pillar — never "everyone."

### Pillar 1 — Categoría: Qué es una Cuenta Asesorada
**Job:** Own the category. Nobody else in LATAM fintech is teaching this.
**Speaks to:** the trust gap — ICP doesn't know CA exists as an option.
**Content codes from the calendar:** CA-1 through CA-5
**Example angle:** "Cuentas Asesoradas: la categoría que nadie en América Latina te había explicado."

### Pillar 2 — Mitos: Lo que crees que te detiene, y no debería
**Job:** Remove the objections before they're spoken out loud.
**Speaks to:** fear of regulatory issues, needing citizenship, complexity.
**Content codes:** MT-1 through MT-5
**Example angle:** "Mito: necesito ser ciudadano americano." → reframe with the real requirement.

### Pillar 3 — Data Breakdown (the ONE core format)
**Job:** The format the plan calls out specifically — market data → what it means for LATAM investors → what Folionet/CA does about it. This is the pillar that builds authority through evidence, not claims.
**Speaks to:** the sophisticated, data-driven side of Profiles 1 and 2 (empresarios, ejecutivos).
**Content codes:** DT-1 through DT-5
**Structure every time:**
1. One real, sourced data point (never a return promise — a market fact)
2. What it means specifically for someone sitting on idle LATAM capital
3. The one-sentence bridge to CA — never a hard sell

### Pillar 4 — Autoridad del Fundador
**Job:** The CEO as a person who has thought deeply about this — not a company account. Builds the trust that drives referrals (the #1 acquisition trigger per the ICP doc).
**Speaks to:** "who is actually behind this" — the trust trigger for registration.
**Content codes:** CEO-1 through CEO-4, plus BK (brecha de conocimiento) and SQ (costo del status quo) as founder-voice variants
**Example angle:** "Por qué fundé Folionet" / "Lo que aprendí en 10 años."
